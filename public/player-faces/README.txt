PLAYER FACE OVERRIDES
=====================

The scoreboard automatically tries to resolve player faces from Wikimedia/Wikipedia.
For broadcast reliability, you can override any player with your own licensed image.

Naming rule:
  John Campbell -> john-campbell.jpg
  Prasidh Krishna -> prasidh-krishna.png
  Nitish Kumar Reddy -> nitish-kumar-reddy.webp

Supported local formats: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp

Local images are always used before online lookup.
Square or portrait images around 500x500 or larger work best.
The overlay crops them automatically.
